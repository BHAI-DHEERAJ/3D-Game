HIDE & HUNT 3D

Hunters vs Props — a 3D prop-hunt party game in your browser

5

How it works

  • 10s hide phase — hunters are locked in the hallway
  • Aim at an object and press E to become it
  • Hunters shoot suspects — hitting real furniture costs 5 HP
  • Every ~20s your prop blasts a tune and gives itself away. Move!
  • Props score for every second alive; hunters win by catching them all

Controls

  • WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump
  • Mouse look · LMB shoot / morph
  • E morph · Q / wheel shuffle disguise
  • F medkit (props) · G grenade (hunters)
  • R reload · Tab scoreboard · Esc mouse

Tip: open this page in a second tab or share the room name with a friend to play together. Bots fill the rest of the lobby.
